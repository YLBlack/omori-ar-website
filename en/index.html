TRANSLATION OF THE GAME OMORI TO ARABIC

Welcome to the project page for OMORI in Arabic. You have been living here for as long as you can remember.
------------------------
Our goal is to to make OMORI more acessible for people in the MENA that do not speak English or French enough to understand what was going on. So we decided to translate the game to Arabic. Right now, translators are working hard to create the best experience.
If you want to know more about the team, we have a TEAM PAGE (still in construction).
Want to download the translation? Sadly, it is not finished, but you can check our progression on the DOWNLOAD PAGE.
Got a question? Check the FAQ PAGE to see if it's answered already.
If you did not find an answer, you can ask in our DISCORD SERVER or you can send us a TWITTER DM!
